Step 1: Open your project folder
1. Press Windows + E to open File Explorer.
2. Open this folder:
   
   C:\Users\ladhr\Industrial-PPE-Detection
3. Click the address bar at the top, type cmd, and press Enter.
A Command Prompt will open in your project folder.
Step 2: Activate your Python environment
Copy and run:
.venv\Scripts\activate


You should see (.venv) at the beginning of the command line.
Step 3: Run your model
To start your live webcam PPE detection, run:
python detect_live.py


Your webcam should open and begin detecting PPE. Press Q while the webcam window is active to close it.
